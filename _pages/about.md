I am a Ph.D. candidate in Machine Learning at [École de Technologie Supérieure (ÉTS)](https://www.etsmtl.ca/) in Montreal, Canada. My research focuses on object detection, vision-language models, test-time and domain adaptation, and video anomaly prediction.

I am currently a visiting Ph.D. researcher at the [University of Trento](https://www.unitn.it/en), Italy, where I am developing training-free methods that use multimodal LLMs to predict whether an anomaly will occur in a video and which people will be involved.

During my Ph.D., I proposed the first test-time adaptation method for vision-language object detectors ([VLOD-TTA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.00458), ECCV 2026) and methods for multi-source domain adaptation in object detection ([PMT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.14950), WACV 2024; [ACIA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.09918), WACV 2025). I also worked on object detection from drone footage as a Mitacs Accelerate intern at Ericsson.
